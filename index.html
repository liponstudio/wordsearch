Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Free to download and play, forever. Ads appear as an optional extra hint and a short full-screen ad after each finished level — that's how a small indie studio keeps the lights on.

Can I play without internet?

The puzzles themselves are fully offline. You only need a connection for the optional rewarded hint and the after-level ad; if no ad is available, you still get the coin bonus.

Will the words ever be impossible?

No. Every generated board is validated before it's shown — every word in the list can be found, forwards or backwards, along a straight line. It's proven in code, not promised in marketing.

Do you collect my data?

We collect nothing. Progress is saved only on your device. Google AdMob may use standard device advertising identifiers to serve ads — see the privacy policy.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets (5.0+). Portrait-first, tuned for one-thumb play.
